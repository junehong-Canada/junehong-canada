## Profile

June Hong is a software architect, technical leader, and innovator focused on
the convergence of **Physical AI, Edge AI, Digital Twins, and LLM systems**.
With more than 20 years of experience across embedded systems, cloud-native
platforms, mobile devices, and AI-driven cyber-physical systems, he designs
reliable software that connects intelligent models with real-world operations.

His current work includes cognitive layers for industrial assets, stateful
Digital Twin models, LangChain and LangGraph agent workflows, retrieval-
augmented generation (RAG), and quantized AI models running on edge hardware.
His career includes engineering and leadership work with organizations such as
Samsung, NTT, NEC, and VTech.

## Core Expertise

* **AI & LLM Systems** — LangChain, LangGraph, LangSmith, RAG pipelines, vector DBs (FAISS, Chroma), TensorFlow/Keras, TFLite (quantization), Scikit-learn.
* **Physical AI & IoT** — Digital Twins, Edge AI, STM32, ESP32, ARM Cortex, Embedded Linux, RTOS (FreeRTOS), industrial protocols (Modbus, BACnet, EtherNet/IP CIP), RS-485, SPI, I2C, UART/USART.
* **Networking & Backend** — TCP/IP, MQTT, RTP/RTCP, VoIP/SIP, WebSocket, FastAPI, Django, Spring Boot, microservices, event-driven architecture.
* **Cloud & DevOps** — AWS (IoT Core, Lambda), Azure, Docker, Redis, PostgreSQL, InfluxDB (time-series), MongoDB, DynamoDB.
* **Frontend & Testing** — React, Tailwind CSS; pytest, Vitest, Unity (embedded) — test infrastructure built from scratch across firmware, backend, and dashboard layers.
